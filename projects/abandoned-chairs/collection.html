Chair

Left Behind

Every chair has a life before it is left behind.

These stories imagine that life, and what follows: the rooms a chair once belonged to, the people it held, and its wandering days on the street. Told from each chair’s point of view, they follow memories of being needed, the uncertainty of abandonment, and the encounters that become part of a new life.
