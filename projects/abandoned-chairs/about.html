Abandoned Chairs

More Than a Chair

I keep wondering about the people who sat in these chairs. They might have eaten dinner in them or talked across a kitchen table. A chair that once sat in someone's home now sits on a New York sidewalk, where anyone passing by can see it.

Abandoned Chairs puts imagined stories and poems alongside photographs of these chairs. In the stories, the chairs remember being needed and wonder what will happen now that they've been left outside.

A project by Yuyao Wang.
