Abandoned Chairs

Abandoned Chairs pairs photographs of chairs left on New York streets with imagined stories and poems about their lives. A worn seat can suggest years of shared meals and kitchen conversations. On the sidewalk, a chair once part of a private home sits where anyone can see it.

In the stories, the chairs recall being needed and face the uncertainty of being left behind. On the website, words unravel or drift away as you interact with the poems. The collection invites you to spend a little time with objects you might usually walk past and imagine where they have been or where they might go next.

A project by Yuyao Wang.
