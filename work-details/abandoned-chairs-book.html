About Abandoned Chairs Book Design

Abandoned Chairs is a ring-bound publication presented through a recording of the physical book. A chair silhouette and the title introduce the publication on a black cover.

Description

Overlapping green and purple lettering combines decorative script with contrasting type sizes. A white chair silhouette stands against the dark cover, while the exposed rings make the binding a visible part of the design.
