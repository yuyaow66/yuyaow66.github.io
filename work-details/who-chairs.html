About Who Chairs？

Who Chairs? looks at the everyday chairs that often pass unnoticed. Moving through themes of rest, waste, street furniture, and fading use, the publication asks readers to reconsider an object that is usually taken for granted.

Description

Chair photographs and cutouts sit alongside halftone textures, graphic shapes, and large, playful type. Shifting compositions and pastel backgrounds give each section a distinct rhythm, while a foldout map brings the collection back to the city.
