About Home: Interior Evocation

Home: Interior Evocation is a narrow, ring-bound publication. Its cover pairs the title with a central image, presented against a burgundy background in the project recording.

Description

A white cover, delicate red typography, and exposed rings give the book a light, compact appearance. The image sits within generous white space, with the title arranged above and below it.
