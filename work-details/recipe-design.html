About Recipe Design

Recipe Design is a small website dedicated to Dan Huang Su, a flaky pastry filled with red bean paste and salted egg yolk. An introduction to its sweet and savory flavors leads into separate ingredient and preparation pages.

Description

A warm yellow textured background and an oversized display heading give the recipe a welcoming tone. Clear links separate the introduction, ingredient list, and ordered steps, keeping each part of the recipe easy to follow across screen sizes.
