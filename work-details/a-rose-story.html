What a Rose Remembers

ZINE · Book design

What a Rose Remembers begins with an attempt to keep a flower. The rose is photographed and wrapped, but its scent and shape continue to change. Each effort to preserve it also reveals something that cannot be held onto.

Blue-toned photographs, drawings, and fragments of writing fill a ring-bound publication. Petals and water return across pages of different sizes, with layered materials concealing and revealing parts of the images as the pages turn.

“I thought keeping it would feel less like losing it.”
