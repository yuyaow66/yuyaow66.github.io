Ember Fae

Illustrations

A pink-haired figure floats among candles, faces, and buildings that seem to melt into one another. Ember Fae uses soft blues, greens, and pinks to draw these forms together, with small flames marking their edges. The scene feels familiar in pieces but difficult to place as a whole, like remembering a dream after waking.
