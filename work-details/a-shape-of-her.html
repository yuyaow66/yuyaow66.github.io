A Shape of Her

Web design

A Shape of Her looks at the words Chinese women hear about how they should live. Remarks about being a good girl, getting married, or caring for a family can stay with a person long after they are first heard.

The online archive holds interviews with my mother, grandmother, and aunts alongside family photographs and research into films and books. Collected remarks are handwritten on torn pieces of paper and can be browsed by life stage or theme. Familiar phrases return across the archive, alongside the different ways women describe their own lives.
