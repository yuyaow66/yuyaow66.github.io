A Shape of Her

Web design

A Shape of Her asks how Chinese women distinguish what they want from what they have learned to want. The research follows everyday remarks that tell a woman how she should live. A reminder to be a good girl can become a question about marriage later in life, or an expectation that she will put her family first.

Interviews with my mother, grandmother, and aunts sit alongside portrayals of Chinese women in film and literature. Their accounts leave room for both happiness and regret: caring for a family can have personal meaning while leaving little time for oneself. In the online archive, family photographs accompany the conversations, and collected remarks appear handwritten on torn paper. Browsing by life stage or theme reveals recurring expectations without losing the differences in how women respond to them.
