About A Shape of Her

A Shape of Her asks how changing social expectations shape Chinese women’s identities and choices throughout their lives. At its center is a personal question: when a woman imagines the life she wants, whose expectations have shaped that desire?

Description

Beginning with women in my own family, I explore how the roles of daughter, wife, and mother become part of a woman’s sense of self. Their stories hold care alongside exhaustion, contentment alongside regret, and independence alongside obligation. The project considers how women recognize these inherited expectations and negotiate space for their own wishes.
