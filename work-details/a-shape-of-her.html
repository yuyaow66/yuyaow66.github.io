About A Shape of Her

A Shape of Her asks how Chinese women separate their own wishes from the expectations they grew up with. I spoke with my mother, grandmother, and aunts about childhood, marriage, work, and family. The archive also includes studies of women in film and literature.

Description

Everyday remarks are handwritten on scraps of paper in Collected Words. Visitors can sort them by life stage, source of pressure, or theme, from obedience and appearance to independence and motherhood. On the interview pages, family photographs accompany each woman’s account.
