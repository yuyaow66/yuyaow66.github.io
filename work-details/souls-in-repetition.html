About Souls in Repetition

Souls in Repetition is a digital zine based on Antonio Porchia’s Voces. It takes up his idea of a soul being worn down again and again, looking at what people give up to fit in and the choices they make along the way.

Description

The website is divided into three sections: The Essence of Souls, The Soul Killer, and Choices and Consequences. Flowers represent the souls, while a dark figure takes the role of the soul killer. In the final section, five buttons each reveal a different line from Porchia’s writing. The illustrated zine can also be opened and read page by page.
