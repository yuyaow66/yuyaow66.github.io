Souls in Repetition

Web design

Souls in Repetition draws on Antonio Porchia’s Voces to ask what a person gives up in order to belong to a collective. The digital zine follows the repeated pressures that can wear away a sense of self.

Individual souls appear as flowers, vulnerable but capable of enduring. A shadowy figure embodies the forces that wear them down, and repeated images make the erosion of individuality visible. Readers make choices that reveal different quotations from Porchia’s writing.
