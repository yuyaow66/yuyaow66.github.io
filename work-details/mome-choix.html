About Môme Choix

Môme Choix is a children’s clothing brand shaped by the small rituals and memories of growing up. Its approach brings together everyday comfort and the warmth of hand-drawn design, creating clothes that can become a familiar part of family life.

Description

I wanted the website to feel warm and easy to browse. Large photographs show the clothes in everyday life, with room to notice their prints and textures. I kept the layout simple so people can focus on the clothes and find what they need.
